# Community Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of the Instigate Cafe Bot, we are committed to fostering a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for all campus students and open-source contributors.

## Our Standards
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, backgrounds, and experiences.
- Gracefully accept and offer constructive feedback.
- Prioritize the safety, privacy, and confidentiality of student community members.
- Never commit or disclose private student records, personal server lore, or credentials.

## Responsibilities & Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and upholding our standards of acceptable behavior. Instances of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior may be reported confidentially to the maintainer at **`imsukesan@gmail.com`**. Maintainers will review and resolve all reports promptly and respectfully.
